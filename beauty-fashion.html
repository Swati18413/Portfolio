How beauty and fashion brands win her, from first look to repeat purchase

I’m fascinated by the intersection of consumer behaviour, technology, and brand building. From beauty and skincare to fashion and lifestyle, I’m curious about what makes people discover, trust, choose, and stay loyal to a brand.

With a Computer Science background and close to a year of Product experience, I bring together technical understanding, product thinking, and a consumer-first mindset. I enjoy identifying everyday friction points, uncovering unmet customer needs, and translating insights into meaningful product experiences.

I believe great products go beyond solving problems — they create connections, build trust, and become part of people’s everyday lives. I want to build products that people don’t just use, but genuinely love.

These are self-initiated concept projects built from public information. They are not affiliated with, or commissioned by, the brands featured.
